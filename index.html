THE STORY · FORT KOCHI

A CORNER
WITH A STORY.

Long before the café became a place to sit, drink and do nothing, Loafer's Corner was already part of the story of Fort Kochi.

01

PRINCESS STREET

BEFORE THE CAFÉ, THERE WAS THE STREET.

Princess Street is one of the earliest streets constructed in Fort Kochi. Its European-style residences and colonial architecture still give the street its old-world character.

Loafer's Corner sits at the meeting point of Princess Street, Peter Celli Street and Bastion Street — a traditional gathering place in the heart of Fort Kochi.

02

THE NAME

WHY
"LOAFER'S"?

One local account, shared by the café's owner, connects the name to a ladies' hostel that once stood in the area.

According to the story, men who gathered around the street were described as "loafers" — people with nowhere particular to be.

A local story passed down around Fort Kochi.

03

TODAY

THE ART OF LOAFING.

The café took that old name and turned it into an invitation.

Come in. Sit down. Take your time. Look out onto the old streets of Fort Kochi. Have a coffee. Stay a little longer than planned.
